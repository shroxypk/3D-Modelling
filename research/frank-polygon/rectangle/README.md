# Cylinder and rectangular intersection review

Source: https://frankpolygon.artstation.com/blog/o7Pg/sub-d-modeling-cylinder-and-rectangle-intersections-fillets

Article text and all six saved diagrams visually reviewed in the renewed study. No completed reconstruction yet.

The sequence contrasts crowded support near a curved junction with a layout planned around the parent cylinder's existing segments. Place the protrusion and support spacing together, rather than adding tight loops after the silhouette is established. The construction diagrams establish the block, route the intersection, add support and inspect subdivision. Later examples vary the root fillet and corner softness.

Transfer test: hold the intended cylinder and protrusion dimensions fixed; compare segment alignment and support spacing under the same reflection. Measure parent-curvature disturbance near the root. More loops alone do not resolve poor placement. The diagrams include cap regions and junctions that are not all quads; the user's quad preference requires considered adaptation, not a claim that the source is uniformly quad-based.

Local files `SubD_Cylinder_Intersection_1.jpg` through `6.jpg` preserve the visual sequence; `manifest.json` records source assets. Practical validation remains pending.
