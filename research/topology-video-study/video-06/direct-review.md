## Decisions to carry forward

- Establish the shape before choosing detail density. Determine whether curvature can support the smallest modeled feature before cutting it in.
- Keep useful regular strips. Redirect only for a reason, and keep the transition out of a sensitive highlight wherever possible.
- Choose local sharpness from the reference. Creases during a density-building stage and support bands for final softness solve different problems.
- Check both the base cage and evaluated surface. Auto-smooth or a material change cannot repair incorrect geometry.
- Use symmetry within individual regions; joining object names alone does not weld topology.
