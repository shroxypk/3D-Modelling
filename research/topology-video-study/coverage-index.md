## What this supports

The 10 files now have visual samples inspected across their timelines, and seven have transcript coverage. The sample rates range from 30 to 60 seconds; this is not continuous playback or a frame-by-frame audit. Three recordings have no complete transcript, so the spoken explanation in those remains unreviewed. The summaries identify visible techniques and useful principles, but they do not establish that every edit operation was reproduced or that the full body of knowledge is mastered.

The most useful shared rule is purpose-led topology: establish the intended silhouette and surface first; trace loops along the features that need them; allow density to change locally; then validate with subdivision and clean reflections before adding only the sharpness the design calls for. Quads are useful where they carry the surface, but a quad count alone does not prove good flow, and a planar n-gon or triangle can be acceptable when shading and downstream edits allow it. For this user's requested modeling style, prefer well-routed quads when practical without adding global loops solely to satisfy a face-count rule.
